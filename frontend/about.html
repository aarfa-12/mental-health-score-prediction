About MindSense AI

AI-Powered Mental Health Assessment

MindSense AI is an AI-powered assessment platform designed to evaluate lifestyle, social media usage, stress, and wellness using a Machine Learning model.

Start Assessment

What is MindSense AI?

Machine Learning

The platform uses a Machine Learning model to generate a mental health score from the information provided during the assessment.

Explainable AI

SHAP-based insights help show the factors that influenced the generated prediction.

Personalized Recommendations

Recommendations are generated from the reported habits and factors that influenced the prediction.

Prediction History

Previous predictions can be viewed through the prediction history section.

How It Works

1

Input

Enter lifestyle and wellness-related information.

2

Preprocessing

The backend prepares the submitted information for the prediction model.

3

Prediction

The Machine Learning model generates a mental health score.

4

Insights

SHAP insights and personalized recommendations help explain the result.

What You Can Get

Mental Health Score

Receive an estimated score based on the information provided in the assessment.

Contributing Factors

Explore the factors that influenced the model's prediction.

Recommendations

Get suggestions based on reported habits and prediction-related factors.

PDF Report

Download a report containing the prediction results and recommendations.

Important Disclaimer

MindSense AI is an assessment and educational project. Its generated score, explanations, and recommendations should not be considered a medical diagnosis or a replacement for professional mental health advice.
